TUSK

Brand Photography

A visual exploration for TUSK, developing a photography language that gives the electric toothbrush a distinctive and elevated identity.

Introduction

Finding a photographic identity for an everyday object.

TUSK is a smart electric toothbrush from riidl, a startup building everyday products with a sharper design sensibility. This project set out to give the product its own photography language — one that could carry it across product shots and film.

What
TUSK brand photography
Role
Visual direction / photography
Output
Product photography + advertising film
Visual Exploration

Testing moods, colour, and light.

Before settling on a direction, the process moved through a range of colour and lighting treatments — looking for what matched the spirit of the product.

Test Shots

Pushing the product against very different settings.

Early test shots ranged from dramatic and minimal to warm and lived-in, checking how the product read in each.

Light

Testing contrast and product visibility.

Context

Exploring how the product could live within a bathroom/lifestyle environment.

Direction

Two concepts, narrowed to a direction.

The experimentation narrowed to two colour directions — a dark, charcoal-toned styling with a sharp, electric edge, and a cobalt-blue, bathroom-countertop styling with a fresher, everyday feel.

Final Photography

The chosen direction, photographed.

Learnings

What the shoot taught along the way.

01

Narrowing the Direction

Too many initial ideas made it difficult to converge. Brainstorming sessions and mood boards helped merge them into one cohesive concept.

02

Working with Equipment

Lighting, camera settings, and equipment required experimentation, along with coordinating shared gear with other teams.

03

Time

A detailed shot list and scheduled time blocks for each section of the shoot helped keep it on track.

Looking Ahead

A better-planned schedule, more time set aside for concepts, equipment checks, and props, and clearer creative direction through mood boards and storyboards from the outset.
